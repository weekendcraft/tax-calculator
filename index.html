Tax rates, rules & FAQ
Estimates for education only, not tax advice. SG: IRAS resident brackets “From YA 2024 onwards”; YA 2026 personal rebate (60%, cap S$200) auto-applied. CPF relief only for Citizen/PR (employee rates by age band, OW ceiling YA2027 S$8,000/mo, YA2026 S$7,400/mo, annual cap S$37,740); PRs in their first 1–2 years pay graduated rates (not modelled). Dependant reliefs: spouse S$2,000 (living with you, spouse income ≤S$8,000), QCR S$4,000/child, parent S$9,000 staying / S$5,500 not staying — amounts may be shared between claimants, enter your share. Non-residents: employment income at 15% flat or resident rates, whichever is higher; no reliefs; other income 24%. Sources: IRAS, CPF. MY: LHDN YA2024+ brackets (0–30%), individual relief RM9,000, EPF employee 11% (60+: 5.5%) with relief capped RM4,000, RM400 rebate if chargeable ≤ RM35,000; non-residents flat 30%, no reliefs. Sources: LHDN/Hasil. ID: PPh 21 Pasal 17 UU HPP (5/15/25/30/35%), PTKP Rp54 jt + Rp4,5 jt kawin + Rp4,5 jt/tanggungan (maks 3), biaya jabatan 5% maks Rp6 jt/th, iuran pensiun deduktibel (BPJS Kesehatan tidak deduktibel); potongan bulanan Jan–Nov memakai TER (PP 58/2023) — kalkulator ini menampilkan angka tahunan. Sumber: DJP.
